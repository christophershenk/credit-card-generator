Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Random Credit Card Generator handles information when you use this website.

Generated card data

Card numbers, cardholder names, security codes, and expiration dates are generated locally in your browser. The website does not send generated card data to a server, save it to a database, or associate it with you.

Technical data and hosting

This website is hosted and protected by Cloudflare. Like most hosting and security providers, Cloudflare may process standard technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP addresses, browser and device information, requested URLs, timestamps, and security events. Cloudflare handles this information under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage, such as page visits, referral sources, approximate location, device and browser information, and basic interactions. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies for measurement. The site operator does not send generated card numbers, cardholder names, security codes, or expiration dates to Google Analytics.

Cloudflare may also use strictly necessary technologies to operate, secure, and deliver the website. Information handled by Google and Cloudflare is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Feedback submissions

The feedback link opens a form provided by Tally. If you choose to submit the form, Tally processes and stores the information you enter on behalf of the site operator. Feedback is used to review issues, respond when possible, and improve the website. Please do not submit passwords, payment information, real card details, or other sensitive personal information.

Feedback may remain in Tally until it is no longer reasonably needed or is deleted. You can use the same feedback form to request access to or deletion of information you previously submitted. Include enough context to help locate the relevant submission.

External services and links

This website links to services operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies:

  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Tally Privacy Notice

Your choices and requests

Using the card generator does not require an account or the submission of personal information. Sending feedback is optional. For a privacy question or request, use the feedback form.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its data practices, or the services it uses change. The latest version and its update date will remain available on this page.
